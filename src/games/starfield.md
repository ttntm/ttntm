Ever since Freelancer all those years ago, I was craving that "space sim RPG" feeling. Elite: Dangerous came close, and I spent many hours on it, but it was lacking the RPG elements.

As such, I felt excited when Starfield was announced back in they day, but it wasn't released for PlayStation at launch. So, I forgot all about it, until it showed up in the PS Store out of nowhere in April 2026. It took half a trailer for me to fall for it completely, just what I needed at that time.

About half a year later, I've played more hours than I care to admit, and I had a really good time. I finished the game, did most of the quests it offered, finished both DLCs, and went to NG+. But most importantly, I built a lot of spaceships. And especially that "lego for gamers" activity of building your very own spaceships got me - leveling up my character to have access to all the parts, earning enough money for additional ship builds, testing them out, and taking screenshots of the ships in photo mode has offered me some much needed escape from the real world's craziness.

Oh, and there's mod support, which is really cool too.
