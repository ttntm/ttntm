Guess it's true that the amount of guitars one needs will always be `n + 1`, haha. Anyway, a lucky find on a second hand platform that I was monitoring for some weeks.

It's a 2015 model, made by Cor-Tek (aka Cort), offering decent craftsmanship that takes it well into the solid mid-range when paired with a proper setup. I paid 80 Euro for it, and spent about two hours on the setup.

What I did:

- Cleaned thoroughly
- Polished the frets
- Oiled the fretboard
- New strings
- Custom tremolo block (sanded down my own wood block)
- Complete setup (neck relief, action, intonation, pickup height)

...all according to {% ext "the manufacturer's official setup guide", "https://support.fender.com/hc/en-us/articles/42584764005019-How-do-I-set-up-my-Stratocaster-guitar-properly" %}.

I'm very pleased with the outcome. The guitars plays _very_ nicely, sounds great when played over my THR10, and provides the iconic single coil sound (aka _Strat twang_) that I was looking for recently.
