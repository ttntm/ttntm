This post covers versions [4.2](/changelog/#4-2) to [4.5.1](/changelog/#4-5-1).

## Content and Pages

This section provides an overview of content and pages with details on what was added, changed and/or (re)moved.

Removed the notes page, [archived it as a post](/blog/archived-notes/), and removed the RSS feed for that collection. I didn't publish new entries for a while, and I lost the motivation to pick up that kind of content again. Bots and scrapers hammering this site for months didn't help much either.

Retired the ideas page, and created the [/backlog](/backlog/). No reason to publish and maintain a list of ideas on this website that I'm never going to work on. The better ones that I keep thinking about are kept in Obsidian these days anyway.

Removed the whisky page, 'cause I stopped collecting bottle. these days, I have an open bottle of something I like around, and that lasts me about a year or even longer right now. The log I had on this website has moved into my Obsidian vault, using their (Data) "Bases" feature.

## Features and Functionality

Additional features and changes enhancing the website's functionality.

Nothing to report, this site feels awfully close to "feature complete" these days.

## Look and Feel

I updated tag/year navigation on the blog archive page, because it felt too cluttered. There's more air now, and I like it better that way.

Sections on the homepage were updated at some point too, due to the fact that the notes page, and the matching collection of entries, were retired.

The background of the mobile navigation bar was updated, which makes it consistent with the - pinned - desktop nav bar. Nothing big, but sometimes it's the little details one's got to work on (aka it was bothering me when looking at the site on a phone).

And finally, back in February, a new font - _Anaheim_ - was introduced for headings and various UI elements. There was nothing wrong with the old one, but it felt like a good time to switch to a new one after all those years.

## Setup and Other Technical Stuff

I updated Eleventy to v3 back in January, before it stopped being called Eleventy. I guess I'll stay on this version until switching SSG at some point, which is an inevitability for any serious SSG user anyway.

Also, automated outgoing webmentions are probably dead, because Echofeed, which I was (ab)using for that, was shut down recently. I updated the architecture chart in the [appendix](/appendix), but didn't do anything else about that. It's probably nothing anyone's noticed yet. I'll consider repairing it at some point (unlikely), or forget it ever existed (very likely).
